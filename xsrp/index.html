xsrp

Xbox Series Remote Play

Tool to Play Any Controller on Xbox

This tool is for better gaming and handle of any controller on Xbox Series X or S. It's inspired from XStreaming. Let's see...

Considerations About the Program

This tool is used to play on Xbox Series X with remote play enable. It return automatically to your view consoles after closing the stream. For toggle fullscren, just press the keyboard key F11. If you want a workaround about using any controller on console with remote play enable and with SIGIL, check my guide for it. To note, if you encounter issue, just delete program folder from %AppData%. And also for using a virtual Xbox controller on top of another controller, you can connect firstly a virtual Xbox controller, and then launch a stream before connect another controller, but prefer use HidHide. To note, it's for a resolution set in 720p. If you encounter issue with overlay asking settings but disabling controls, press each time Xbox button to take again controls. Also better use a Xbox One wired controller without Bluetooth connectivity, connected to your console to avoid annoying shut down of this one (from Nacon is a good one). And also, if you want to know why a console is terrible, check my guide for it.
