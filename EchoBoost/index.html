EchoBoost

Echo of Sound

Peripheral Forward of Sound

This program is an echo of sound able to forward sound simultaneously on several sound peripherals. There isn't any latency. Let's see...

Considerations About the Program

If you have external bluetooth speakers to ear sound and a sound barre on your laptop PC to ear virtual sound, it's a must have. You need to enable both audio cards from sound settings. It's a success after several attempts like with EchoEqualizer, or Echorce, or EchoMic, or Echo. If you are using it as an equalizer, you just need to set your internal speakers as default device and communication device, and for it to set a level of earing of 0, having a virtual sound equalized for your external speakers, and for both to set a quality of 24 bits, 48 000 Hz, in order to not ear sound cracking sometimes. If you aren't using external bluetooth speakers, there will be a latency.
