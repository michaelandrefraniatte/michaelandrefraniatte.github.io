Guide

Armor-X Pro

Configuring Armor-X Pro

You need to connect to your PC or console the Armor-X Pro wired or with the dongle after inserted Armor-X Pro in your Xbox controller. With joy.cpl on PC, open with shortcut Win + R, by typing it, you can see how it's running. By default the gyro is too sensitive. You need to download the BigBig Won retailer application under Google store running on a Android smartphone. For scanning your controller, you need to allow Bluetooth, WiFi, and localization (GPS) under settings of your smartphone and settings of the application. It need to set your modem/router firewall to medium. Now, it's time to setup it.

Setting Armor-X Pro

After scanning your controller under the BigBigWon retailer application for your Armor-X Pro, you enter in settings of Armor-X pro. The sensitivity of 0 is the lowest you can. The linear curve is flawless. You can set a compensation deadzone to not have flaw with the deadzone of games for your stick, in order gyro have fine aim in center, i.e. when you moving a little with gyro. You can set a button for toggle gyro aiming.

Playing Competitive Now on Console and PC

I advice to be used to play with your fingers while aiming, because gyro is just a help for aiming. I don't advice to tweak gyro for a ratchet control to aim like with a stick, where you need to recenter in front of you the controller for stoping moving the view, because you will not be able to aim with the stick. Prefer for it a Playstation controller, moreover the gyro in axis Y is drifting, because the floor isn't good enough. Maybe there are settings for it, in the application, but I'm not be used to it, so I don't know. I think gyro is less sensitive when you moving up, than down, or left or right. You can play a lot of FPS and TPS games on PC and console with Armor-X Pro aiming with gyro and stick. To be competitive you need to practice a lot. But it's kinda funny.
