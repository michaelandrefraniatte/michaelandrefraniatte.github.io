MIC2EDIT
Set Your Speech as Input Text!
GitHub Doc Extension Privacy
It's made to use your microphone as speech to text written for any website like Youtube or Twitch, etc. It writting your speech under input divs. To note, it need to allow your microphone under websites asking to grant access. And also, it need to enable your microphone from sound properties under input card tab (mmsys.cpl).
