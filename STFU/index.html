Spotify Tracks For You

Record Songs over Spotify while You Listen to It

The program is made for capture audio sound from sound card for recording perfectly songs over Spotify. The files recorded are in MP3 format. Let's see...

Considerations About the Program

It stoping capture after the end of the current track, and restart before the next track, recording each time a song in a new file. It automatically records the file with the good name, and with tags like title and artist of song. It's the features of rmomo and AlbumCapture but in a better shape for record easy songs and add it directly to DesktopAudio or DesktopOverlay. You just need to trigger twice the record for starting but after it trigger alone the record for each song you listen with right flow. If the name of title is too long it need to use rmomo to change title's tag. You having a far better audio sound listening with the mp3 recorded. You may need to know the process in order to delete wrong MP3 files... First it open the folder where it record, then when you start to listen a song, it record in WAV format, then it convert to MP3 format, and it delete just after the WAV format. When you listen a different song, it start a new record, from artist and title and from empty audio sound. Prefer to let open the folder to manage your records, and also do not let your PC to shut down. To record properly, the level of earing must be to 100% from audio card settings, so use speakers for not your sound would be too loud. It's used for my DesktopOverlay directly and only by copy/paste recorded mp3 files. If the name of title is too long it need to use rmomo to change title's tag. You having a far better audio sound listening with the mp3 recorded.
