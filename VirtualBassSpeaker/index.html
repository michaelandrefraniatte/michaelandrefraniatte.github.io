VirtualBassSpeaker

Equalizer and Amplifier of Sound

Universal Equalizer Forwarding Sound

This program is an equalizer and amplifier of sound able to forward sound simultaneously on several sound peripherals. The main goal was to predict where come from enemies easier, by earing footsteps amplified as a footsteps amplifier for FPS games. Let's see...

Considerations About the Program

It's for use as a bass speaker, enabling to ear sound on multiple speakers. It's made if you have a laptop PC and external speakers as default peripheral for listen music. The internal speakers of your laptop PC are used as bass speaker. Both external and internal speakers must be enable, and level of earing well adapted. If you encounter issue while using the program, you can delete tempsave file under same folder of the program. If you set default speakers and level of earing to 0, the internal speakers of your laptop PC, the program task as an equalizer. By the what, you can have several Bluetooth speakers connected to your laptop PC, and then forward like this the sound by opening several times the program and choosing which speakers to control the sound out, in order to listen the sound simultaneously on several speakers, without the need of other program or method that would be invasive. If you have external speakers to ear virtual sound and a sound barre to virtualize bass, it's a must have. To note, the changes in equalizer settings take effect when you click on the start button, not in real time. It's a success after several attempts like with ModernEqualizer, or SoundBooster, or UniversalEqualizer. To note, there's a file for parameters named params.txt. You can close the program from notify icon by clicking right on it. You can open the equalizer from notify icon by double clicking on it. But take care, if your default speakers is not your laptop speakers to virtualize sound for bass speaker, set a level of earing for EchoBoost to 0.10f - 0.20f, not to 1.00f if you virtualize sound in your Bluetooth speakers for using it as an equalizer. Otherwise the sound would be very loud. It needs to enable Stereo Mixer input card from sound properties, listening default output card, levels of sound for both cards must be set to minimum (reaching 0), or if your default output card is connected to a device, you can lower the sound from the device, and listen with other device you choose.
