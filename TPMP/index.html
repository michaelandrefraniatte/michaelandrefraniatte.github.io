Listen Music on Your Computer

The program is made for listen all your music present on your computer in "songs" folder. It's simple to use and very intuituve. Let's see...

Considerations About the Program

The program is simple to use and can be used on a simple pocket PC. Check my program named rmomo for creation of audio tags. To get notified, go to Settings > System > Notifications & actions, and turn on "Get notifications from apps and other senders". If you add mp3 files in the folder you chosen, you need to choose the folder again to listen to your music with mp3 files added.
